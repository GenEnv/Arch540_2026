# Design notes: Kingsway housing (studio, draft 2)

- site: corner of E Kingsway and a lane, near Fraser St. about 1,800 sqm. falls to the east, 2.4 m over the length
- mid-rise, 8 storeys. wood frame over a concrete podium (or all mass timber? undecided)
- 96 units. mix: 40% studio and 1BR, 40% 2BR, 20% 3BR (family units)
- ground floor: shops on Kingsway, 4 townhouse-style units on the lane side
- big idea: open a courtyard to the south-west so it gets afternoon sun and pulls light into the deep floor plate
- courtyard 14 m wide, raised one level above the street, shared by all units
- double-loaded corridors are dark, so split the building into two bars, bridges link them at levels 3 and 6
- 9-storey block on the Kingsway side steps down to 5 storeys at the lane (neighbours are 3-storey houses)
- parking: 1 level underground, 48 stalls, 190 bike spaces
- FSR about 3.2, check zoning
- facade: brick? reuse brick from the warehouse next door, maybe. ask about cost
- energy: passive house target, heat pump, PV on roof
- need to test: shadow on the lane houses at 3 pm, equinox
- critique last week: too many ideas, pick one
