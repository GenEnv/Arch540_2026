# Planning rules

1. Setback: every building stays at least 3 m inside the lot boundary.
2. Sun gap: where one building sits north of another and their east-west extents overlap, the gap between them (measured north-south, wall to wall) must be at least 0.7 x the height of the southern building.
3. Spacing: any two buildings are at least 12 m apart (shortest distance between their walls).
4. Height: at most 40 m. Each type also keeps to its storey range.
5. Open space: at least 25 % of the lot area is left unbuilt.
