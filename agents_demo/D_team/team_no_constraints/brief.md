# Brief: corner block next to the park (fictional site)

Compiled from the developer's kickoff notes, the city's pre-application letter, and a later email. Some parts repeat, some are loose.

## Site
- About 100 m (east-west) by 70 m (north-south), 7,000 m2. Flat.
- Streets on the south side and the east side. A public park directly north of the site. Neighbouring building to the west.
- Coordinates used by all tools: x runs east from the west edge (0 to 100), y runs north from the south street edge (0 to 70). The park starts at y = 70.

## Program (developer kickoff notes)
- "We are aiming for 220 to 280 homes."
- Unit mix target: studio 10%, one-bedroom 35%, two-bedroom 35%, three-bedroom 20%. The city accepts plus or minus 5 percentage points on each.
- Net unit areas used in the pro forma: studio 33 m2, 1b 50 m2, 2b 75 m2, 3b 98 m2. Net area is 82% of floor area (the rest is corridors, stairs, walls).
- Ground-floor retail along the south street: at least 1,200 m2, touching the south frontage.
- Daycare for 37 children. Own one-storey building of at least 260 m2. Outdoor play area of at least 259 m2 (7 m2 per child). The operator says the play area "needs real sun, mornings and around midday".
- A shared courtyard of at least 400 m2 (open, not covered by any building).
- We care about: how many homes we deliver, how many family (three-bedroom) homes, daycare play-area sun, keeping the park unshaded, and construction cost.

## City rules (pre-application letter)
- FSR (floor space ratio, gross floor area divided by site area) cap 2.8. All floor area above ground counts, including retail and the daycare.
- Height cap 40 m. Within the 30 m x 30 m corner zone at the south-east corner (x from 70 to 100, y from 0 to 30) up to 55 m is allowed.
- Setbacks from the lot line: west 2 m, south 3 m, east 3 m, north 4 m.
- Any two buildings taller than 25 m must be at least 25 m apart.
- No new shadow on the park between 10:00 and 14:00 (solar time) at the equinox. The city will tolerate up to 5 m2.
- Floor-to-floor heights: ground floor 4.5 m, upper floors 3.1 m each. The daycare is one storey of 4.0 m.

## Email from the developer's CEO (received after the kickoff)
"The pro forma only works with 250 homes minimum. Please make sure every scheme gets there. I also want as many family units as we can fit, and an iconic tower at the corner, we have the 55 m allowance for a reason. Keep costs sensible."

## Notes from the community meeting
- "Please keep the park sunny."
- "No huge blank wall on the south street."
- Parents at the daycare want the play area away from the street.
